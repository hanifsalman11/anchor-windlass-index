Frequently Asked Questions (26-Foot Offshore Center Console Free-Fall Windlass Sizing)

What size anchor and windlass do I need for 26-Foot Offshore Center Console Free-Fall Windlass Sizing (26 ft boat)?

Per ABYC H-40 windage tables, a 26-foot vessel requires ~1,500 lbs of storm holding power—matched by a 22-lb High-Holding-Power (HHP) anchor. Because total hanging ground tackle weighs ~72 lbs, select a 12V windlass with at least 230 lbs Working Load Limit and 700 lbs Max Pull.

How much anchor chain and nylon rode do I need for a 7:1 scope?

Scope is measured from the bow roller to the seabed ($ ext{Water Depth} + ext{Bow Height}$). In 25 feet of water with a 4-foot bow height (29 ft vertical), a 5:1 day scope requires 145 feet of rode, and a 7:1 overnight storm scope requires 203 feet of rode.

What size battery cable and breaker do I need for a 500W 12V windlass?

A 500W 12V windlass draws ~42 Amps under heavy retrieval load. Because the round-trip cable run from the stern battery bank to the bow locker is typically 40 to 55 feet, run 4 AWG Tinned Marine Copper protected by a 50-Amp manual-reset windlass circuit breaker to keep voltage drop under 10%.
